What is UCMAS?

Our afterschool program is called UCMAS which stands for Universal Concept of Mental Arithmetic System. It was started in Malaysia by Dr.Dino Wong and is now an international program being taught in over 80 countries! In Canada, we have over 100 UCMAS Schools across almost every province. In Quebec, there are 14 locations. No matter where you move, it will be the same curriculum and syllabus. We have over 32 years of experience and 3 million kids+ globally.

Why UCMAS

Even though we teach math, the main goal is whole brain development, which help children excel in all subjects! Your child will attain many lifelong skills. By the end, they will be able to retain information longer, manage time better, study efficiently, stay confident in exams without stress, and could multitask without losing focus. These skills will still be there even after university.
