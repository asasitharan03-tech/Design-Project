About Us

What do we do? In UCMAS we teach students the abacus, which is an ancient chinese calculator. Since the different nerves in your fingers connect to the different lobes in the brain, we use the 6 finger technique. During class we stimulate the brain with various activities to improve the 3 main skills, auditory, listening, and kinesthetic. These activites also improve speed, accuracy, confidence, imagination, observation skills, time management, focus, concentration, listening, memory power, and many more.

In UCMAS we accept children from ages 3 to 13. Each child starts from level 1, but we seperate them into 3 different groups. Ages 3 to 5 is the pre-ucmas group. Ages 5 to 8 is group 1. Ages 9 to 13 is group 2. Check the levels section to see what students learn in each level, and what they learn in each group.

How does the Abacus Develop the brain? Well we use both hands, each using 3 fingers. We call this the 6 finger technique. Because each finger is connected to a different lobe of the brain, this stimulates both sides of the brain. Once they learn the physical abacus, they start imagining it in their mind while doing calculations.
