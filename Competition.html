2026 Competition

Our National Competition is happening on May 31st at Embassy Plaza in Laval. This year in Quebec, we are doing the Visual, Flash, and Listening competition. We have around 1,800 participants in Quebec only.

Visual Competition

Students complete 200 questions in 8 minutes, the questions depend on their level!

Flash Competition

Students will be doing questions flashed on the big screen, questions will progressively get bigger. Questions vary depending on their modules. It goes on until there is only one student left, and they will be the winner.

Listening Competition

Students will be doing questions that are called out through an app, like the flash competition, questions will get harder and vary depending on their module. It goes on until there is only one student left.
